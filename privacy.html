Privacy Policy

PetPill
Effective Date: March 18, 2026 · Last Updated: March 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how PetPill ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application and related services, including our iOS widget and Apple Watch companion app (collectively, the "Services").

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described herein, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

The App collects only the information you voluntarily enter to manage your pet's medication schedule:

  • Pet Information: Name, species, photo, and optional notes.
  • Medication Information: Medication name, dosage amount and unit, medication type, frequency/schedule, start and end dates, and optional notes.
  • Dose Logs: Timestamps of scheduled and administered doses, dose status (e.g., given, skipped, missed), and optional notes.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, request, or store any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, or mailing address)
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, or similar)
  • Location or geolocation data
  • Health data (the App does not integrate with Apple HealthKit)
  • Contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera data
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers

2.3 Purchase Information

If you subscribe to PetPill Pro or purchase a lifetime license, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat") as a third-party service to verify your purchase entitlements. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous, randomly generated user identifier (not linked to your Apple ID or personal identity)
  • Purchase receipt data (product identifiers, transaction status, subscription expiry)

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, Apple ID, or payment method details. For more information, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described in Section 2 solely to:

  • Display and manage your pet medication schedules within the App
  • Deliver local notifications to remind you of upcoming doses
  • Synchronize your data across your personal devices via Apple iCloud
  • Enable optional family sharing so household members can coordinate pet care
  • Verify your subscription or purchase status to unlock premium features
  • Display medication information in the App's widget and Apple Watch companion

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core functionality of the App.

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 On-Device Storage

All App data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework in an encrypted SQLite database. The database resides within the App's sandboxed container and benefits from iOS file-level encryption (Data Protection).

4.2 iCloud Synchronization

If you have iCloud enabled on your device, the App uses Apple's CloudKit framework to synchronize your data across your personal devices signed into the same Apple ID. This data is transmitted to and stored on Apple's iCloud servers. Apple encrypts data in transit and at rest.

The App does not operate its own servers. We do not have direct access to your iCloud data. Your data is governed by Apple's iCloud Terms and Conditions and Apple's Privacy Policy.

4.3 App Group Shared Storage

The App shares a limited data container with its widget extension and Apple Watch companion to enable dose display and logging from these surfaces. This data remains on your device and within Apple's sandboxed environment.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 Family Sharing (User-Initiated)

The App offers an optional family sharing feature that allows you to share a pet's medication data with other iCloud users you designate. When you initiate sharing:

  • The invited individual receives read and write access to the shared pet's records, including pet information, medications, and dose logs.
  • Sharing is facilitated through Apple's CloudKit sharing infrastructure. Participant information (name and email associated with the participant's Apple ID) is managed by Apple.
  • You may revoke a participant's access at any time.

We do not control, access, or store participant identity information. Family sharing is governed by Apple's CloudKit and iCloud terms.

5.3 No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information or App data to any third party for any purpose.

5.4 Legal Disclosure

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

6. Notifications

The App requests your permission to send local notifications for medication dose reminders. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on your device. No notification data is transmitted to our servers or any third party.

You may disable notifications at any time through your device's Settings.

7. Photos

The App allows you to attach a photo to each pet profile using Apple's system photo picker (PhotosPicker). The App accesses only the specific photo you select — it does not access your full photo library. Selected photos are stored locally within the App's database and may be synchronized via iCloud as described in Section 4.2.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect personal identification information from any user, no age verification mechanism is implemented.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at the address in Section 13 so we can take appropriate action.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

9.1 Retention

Your data is retained on your device and in iCloud for as long as you use the App. We do not independently retain any copy of your data.

9.2 Deletion

You may delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual records (pets, medications, or dose logs) within the App
  • Deleting the App from your device, which removes all locally stored data
  • Disabling iCloud sync or deleting the App's iCloud data through your device's Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage

Because we do not maintain independent servers or databases, deletion of your iCloud data removes all copies under our control.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You may view all data stored in the App at any time through the App interface.
  • Correction: You may edit any record within the App.
  • Deletion: You may delete any or all data as described in Section 9.2.
  • Portability: Your data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is accessible across your Apple devices.
  • Objection/Restriction: Because we do not process data for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making, these rights are not applicable.

For residents of the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or jurisdictions with similar data protection laws: the legal basis for processing your data is the performance of a contract (providing the App's functionality to you) and your explicit consent (for iCloud synchronization and notifications).

For residents of California (USA): under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. As stated in Section 5.3, we do not sell personal information.

11. International Data Transfers

If you use iCloud, your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside your country of residence, in accordance with Apple's data processing practices. We do not independently transfer data across borders. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on their international data handling.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
